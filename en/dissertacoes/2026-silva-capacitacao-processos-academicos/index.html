The people who look after your diploma need training too

Behind every diploma there are records, regulation and coordination teams that must follow Ministry of Education rules that change all the time. The dissertation listened to 131 of these professionals and, based on what they said, designs a platform to train them: GENESIS360. Explained in 12 screens.

04 The method

The author heard from 131 professionals, then 69 more

The method is Design Science Research: instead of only describing a problem, the research designs a solution (an "artifact") for it. The starting point was two online questionnaires.

Stage 1 · diagnosis
Invitation to 200 professionals through WhatsApp groups, e-mail and LinkedIn, between June and July. 131 answered (65.5%): profile, daily dealings with the rules, difficulties and what they would think of a platform.
Stage 2 · the modules
Invitation to 150 institutions. 69 answers (46%) rated from 1 to 5 how important each of the eight modules planned for the platform would be.

10 The proposal

Try out the idea behind GENESIS360

An app (and later a website) that an institution subscribes to in order to train its team: courses in learning paths by role, rules always up to date, checklists and a dashboard for managers. Pick a role on the phone and look around.

Our illustration · the platform does not exist yet

11 Application and evaluation

The platform has targets but no results yet

The dissertation ends with a plan: test GENESIS360 in three cycles and measure six indicators. The author herself calls these numbers hypothetical benchmarks, not results.

1Working prototypebasic learning paths and dashboards (the "minimum viable product")
2Pilotat a partner institution, with support and feedback collection
3Continuous improvementwith data on use, usability and compliance

Business model: a monthly subscription paid by the institution, with a setup fee of R$ 10,000 to 20,000 and a monthly fee of R$ 2,000 to 6,000 (prices "to be confirmed"), plus a version that plugs into the management systems institutions already use. Target: 50 institutions and 3 system partners in two years.

Sections 4.1, 4.2 and 6. The abstract and the final remarks cite "100% compliance" and "a 30% reduction in errors" as expected results; in section 4.2 the author makes clear they are targets to be measured once the platform is tested. The striped band shows that nothing has been measured yet.

12 In practice, limits and credits

What this proposal leaves us

For those who run institutions

For the author, continuously training records, coordination and management teams has become a critical success factor. Depending on a few veterans leaves the institution vulnerable when they leave.

For the teams

Many answers ask for the rules to be translated: turning each rule into step-by-step guidance, with process templates and checklists.

For entrepreneurs

In the author's words, applied innovation is born from listening to real problems, including the silent ones. In highly regulated markets, the edge lies in offering certainty with a simple digital experience.

Next steps

Build the prototype, run the pilot and measure the six indicators; extend the idea to other topics (evaluation, distance learning) and to other Latin American countries.

What this research does not say

  • A proposal, not a product. The platform was designed and prototyped on screen but not yet tested; the indicators are targets. · according to the author (section 4.2)
  • Opinion before use. The usefulness is what people imagined without having used the platform, and the scores pile up at the top of the scale. · ceiling effect pointed out by the author
  • Who answered. The invitation went out through the author's network (WhatsApp, e-mail, LinkedIn), and 87% were from private institutions; the author acknowledges the limited diversity. The profile of the 69 in stage 2 is not described. · author and our note
  • Who proposes it. The research was carried out by the consultancy that will offer the platform (ARMOR), as is common in professional master's programs. · our note
