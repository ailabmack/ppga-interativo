What moves the value of a multimarket fund?

Multimarket funds can invest in interest rates, currencies, stocks and commodities at the same time. The research followed Brazil's 50 largest, day by day, from 2017 to mid-2023, measured how each one performed and looked for the economic indicators that move together with their units. Interest rates weighed the most. Explained in 12 screens.

02 The starting point

A fund that can invest in almost anything

An investment fund pools the money of many people, the unitholders, and hands it to a professional manager. Investors buy units; the value of a unit rises or falls with what the manager did.

1A multimarket fund combines fixed income, currencies, stocks and other markets, and can use derivatives to leverage or hedge the portfolio.
2The result depends on the manager's choices: when to enter each market and how much to put in each.
3They are the Brazilian funds closest to foreign hedge funds. The research studied only open-ended funds, which investors can enter and leave at any time.

04 How to tell whether a fund did well · sections 2.4 to 2.6

Earning more is not enough: you have to see how much risk was taken

The dissertation uses three classic ratios. All three divide the same gain above the CDI (Brazil's benchmark interbank rate) by a different measure of risk: the higher the ratio, the better the trade-off between gain and risk.

Sharpe
0,0gain ÷ total swings
Sortino
0,0gain ÷ downside swings only
Treynor
0,0gain ÷ beta (how much it follows the market)

06 Return and swings · Tables 7 and 8

Swinging more did not pay more

Each dot is a fund: how much its units swung per year (to the right, more risk) and how much they returned (up, more gain) over the 12, 24 or 36 months to June 2023. If risk were always rewarded, the dots would rise from left to right.

Correlation between swings and return our calculation: −0.35 over 12 months, −0.60 over 24 and −0.08 over 36. Accumulated return in the window; swings = annualised volatility.

08 The unexpected · Table 5

For funds that barely follow the stock market, the Treynor ratio goes haywire

In the 12- to 36-month windows, the funds' Treynor ranged from −2,991 to +6,482, while the Sharpe stayed between −3.9 and +8.9. The same fund that leads on the Sharpe comes last on the 12-month Treynor.

Our reading The dissertation does not publish the betas. The explanation comes from the formula (section 2.5): the Treynor divides by the beta, and the dissertation itself notes that relying on the beta is a limitation of the ratio. When the beta is close to zero, any gain becomes a huge number; if the beta is negative, the sign flips.

09 How the dissertation measured · sections 5.1 and 5.2

76,000 fund-days against 12 economic indicators

For each fund and each day, the model puts the unit value side by side with twelve indicators. Everything enters in logarithms, so the result reads in percentages: when the indicator is 1% higher, the units tend to be x% higher or lower.

Panel data regression with fund and year fixed effects and Driscoll-Kraay standard errors, which correct the autocorrelation flagged by the Wooldridge test; the Hausman test pointed to fixed effects. 76,434 observations (Table 12). Accumulated Selic: Brazil's policy rate added up day by day, an index that only grows.

12 In practice · limits · credits

When choosing a fund, look at interest rates and at more than one ratio

For investors

Compare funds by risk-adjusted return, over more than one time window, and not only by how much they earned. The author reminds readers that past performance does not guarantee future results. This page is not investment advice.

For fund managers

Monitoring the indicators that weighed in the model, especially interest rates, inflation, commodities and foreign markets, helps anticipate how units react to changes in the economy.

For researchers

Include management and performance fees, each fund's liquidity and risk, test non-linear effects and use a longer horizon or high-frequency data.

What this research does not say

  • Association, not cause. The model compares levels of units and of indices that grow over time; series like these tend to look linked. · our note
  • Only the largest, and the survivors. 50 large funds from 2017; four were closed, and the results may not hold for other multimarket funds. · according to the author, and our note
  • No costs. Management and performance fees were not in the model. · according to the author
  • Numbers to double-check. In Table 12, some standard errors do not match the t-values and two coefficients have a misplaced decimal point; for gold we show only the direction. The bottom groups by Treynor repeat those by Sharpe and were left out. · our note
  • Time changes things. Crises, rule changes and economic cycles can alter the relationships. · according to the author
