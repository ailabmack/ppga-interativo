Master's dissertation · PPGA Mackenzie · 2018

In the field, the bank's agents learn sustainability without leaving the logic of credit

An agribusiness bank hired agronomists to visit the farms it finances. The dissertation heard 11 of these sustainability agents: they learn through four paths and go beyond inspection, but what they bring to farms stays within what the law and credit require, and the actors in the field do not talk to one another.

Author: Tiago Luis Silva Santos Advisor: Prof. Dr. Janette Brunstein Program: Graduate Program in Business Administration (PPGA) · Mackenzie Presbyterian University

He stopped at 11 because the answers were already repeating. The bank is not identified in the dissertation.

12 Takeaways

The agent teaches and learns in the field, but still works more to fix than to change

For the author, the job was created to be proactive but follows a very reactive logic: it prevents a lost harvest, suggests better housing, guides licensing. He proposes a learning curve for the agents and for the bank itself.

For banks and agricultural lenders

  • An agronomist in the field gives the bank information no report provides, say the agents (“I am the bank in the field”).
  • The author suggests the agents take part in industry forums, such as the banking federation's sustainability events, now attended only by office staff.

For agents and field technicians

  • Learning to speak the farmer's language shows up as the biggest lesson for several agents.
  • Less red tape would leave more time to reflect and plan with the farmer, according to the author.

For those thinking about sustainability in farming

  • Rural communities, culture and local politics were left out of the conversations; including them is a step the author points to.
  • Bringing farmer, bank, environmental agencies and associations to one table is what social learning proposes. (our reading of the theory used)

What this research does not say

  • What the farmers think. Only the agents were heard; farmers, industry associations and public bodies were left for future studies. · according to the author
  • Whether it holds for other banks. It is a single bank; the author suggests comparing with the same bank's branches abroad and with other Brazilian agribusiness lenders. · according to the author
  • What actually happens on the farms. The research heard accounts and read documents; it did not follow visits or measure results on the farms. · our observation
  • How much each theme weighs. The themes were not counted; the order and size of the drawings on this page do not measure frequency. · our observation
