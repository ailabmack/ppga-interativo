Family business: who decides and how much the family weighs

In three studies, reviewing 71 articles on small businesses and 231 on family businesses, the thesis shows that strategy tends to run through the owner, proposes a definition of strategic decision-making that includes the family's ties, goals and well-being, and treats the family business as a matter of degree in ownership, on the board and in management, with six reference configurations.

What makes a health plan operator take on debt?

Can you predict a stock's price one minute ahead?

Using the prices of ten of the most traded stocks on B3, minute by minute, in 2022, the thesis compared eight ways of forecasting the next price. ARIMA, a classic statistical model, was among the best for all of them; neural networks, almost never.
