How much can you lose when the market tumbles?

Using prices of seven B3 stocks every 30 seconds in 2022, the dissertation measured extreme losses with VaR and Expected Shortfall: beyond the VaR line, the average fall was at least twice the line. An LSTM neural network, tested on PETR3, could not forecast the losses.

Do firms that generate more cash borrow more?

In 757 companies from six Latin American countries, from 2017 to 2021, the size and instability of cash moved debt, but the direction changed with the yardstick used; in the pandemic, instability started to matter and interest rates stopped mattering.

Financial literacy in small businesses

With 163 owners and managers of small retail and services businesses in Brazil’s Northeast, the thesis shows that getting personal finance questions right was not linked to business decisions; business finance habits, however, went along with negotiating more with suppliers, in a small effect.

The social value of microcredit

Thirty micro-entrepreneurs in Brazil's Northeast describe what microcredit is worth to them. For ten, it keeps the business going and pays for part of life; for twenty, it makes the business grow. Neither way reflects the mission of the company that lends.

A stock exchange inside the computer

With 10,000 virtual investors who copy their neighbours and fear losses, the dissertation simulated each of five B3 stocks 5,400 times: each stock’s best simulation passed the price-direction test, but drifted away from the real price after about 150 trading days and did not reach ARIMA.
