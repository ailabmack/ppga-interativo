Doctoral thesis · PPGA Mackenzie · 2025

When the algorithm joins HR, is it a tool or a colleague?

Dashboards, systems and artificial intelligence reached people management with the promise of data-driven decisions. The thesis interviewed 10 HR directors and business partners in the Brazilian pharmaceutical industry and concludes that this People Analytics acts as an actor: it changes goals, tasks and the professionals themselves. Out of this comes a new crisis, the hybridization crisis. Explained in 12 screens.

02 The question

Tool or actor? It depends on the lens you look through

People Analytics is the use of data, systems and algorithms to decide about people: whom to hire, who may leave, how the climate is. The thesis looks at it through two lenses at once. Switch lenses and pick an aspect.

05 Findings · theme 1, the translation of reality

Before data can create value, it has to convince

10 The finding

A sixth crisis on the companies' staircase: hybridization

Researcher Larry Greiner described organizational growth as a staircase of phases and crises: each period of growth sows the seeds of the next crisis. The thesis proposes a sixth step, algorithmization, and with it a new crisis. Tap the steps.

Figures 20 and 23 and sections 3.3 and 4.3. The thesis uses Greiner's model in its 1998 version. The author presents the sixth phase as a theoretical lens, not as a proven universal stage. Step heights are a diagram.

12 In practice, limits and credits

Use the algorithms without handing them your judgment

HR professionals

Learn to read data critically: know where it comes from, what biases it carries and how the algorithm turns people into numbers. Ask the right questions before asking for answers.

Companies

Data and AI governance with an owner, rules and clear roles. Systems that talk to each other, and pilots before rolling out a tool, as the interviewees learned.

HR leaders

Lead the change: translate the new reality for teams, handle resistance and invest in reskilling. Design processes in which the machine calculates and people interpret.

Everyone

Keep a "humanistic skepticism": use AI as a powerful tool, never as an oracle. Make sure every algorithmic decision can be reviewed and contested by a person.

What this research does not say

  • It only heard decision makers. HR directors and business partners spoke; employees, who are measured by the systems, were left out. The thesis calls for that research.
  • Ten people from one sector. The pharmaceutical industry is strongly hierarchical and heavily regulated; the picture is not a statistic for other sectors.
  • A snapshot of one moment. Tools change fast; the author bets that the human dynamics described will last longer.
  • The hybridization crisis is a theoretical proposal. It organizes what the interviews show, but it was not measured or compared across companies. · our note
  • The quotes are the author's English translations of interviews held in Portuguese. · our note
