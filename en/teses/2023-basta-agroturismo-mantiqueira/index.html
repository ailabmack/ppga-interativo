Doctoral thesis · PPGA Mackenzie · 2023

Why did the farm open its gate to tourists?

During the pandemic, tourists swapped long trips for places close to home. The thesis listened to five farmers in São Bento do Sapucaí, in the Mantiqueira mountains, who grow olives, grapes, mushrooms and vegetables and welcome visitors. Tourism arrived years after the first seedling, helps pay the bills and promotes the product. And the crisis led the small producers to team up and invent products. Explained in 12 screens.

02 The topic · agritourism

Agritourism is the tourist inside the routine of a working farm

In 2019, tourism was worth about 10.4% of Brazil’s GDP. The 2020 lockdown halted the sector and sent tourists to places close to home, which put agritourism in the spotlight. But not every trip to the countryside is agritourism. Where does each of these fit?

04 The lens · strategic action fields

A market is a field where everyone watches each other and contests the rules

For sociologists Neil Fligstein and Doug McAdam, a market is a “strategic action field”: there are those who dominate (incumbents), those trying to rise (challengers), those who oversee the rules (governance units) and the state, which validates the actors. Outside shocks can upset everything. See the three states of a field and what the thesis found in the mountains.

05 Method · life stories

The author went to the mountains twice and heard five farmers’ stories

The method, created by sociologist Daniel Bertaux, asks people to tell their own path with as few interruptions as possible. This reveals the “course of action”: the plan behind each step. Tap the phases.

09 The state and governance units

Government helps people start and weighs on them when it is time to grow

In the accounts, seedlings, technical support and research came during setup. Later, credit that does not recognize the crop, food safety rules made for large industry and poor roads become obstacles. Sebrae, Senac, Senai (Brazil’s small business and vocational training agencies) and local associations help with management and products. Tap a name.

11 The finding · the thesis’s model

Tourism comes in after the crop and helps keep it standing

The model has four phases. The first three build up the farm; tourism starts at the end of setting up, with almost no planning, to sell and promote the product and cover part of the costs. During maintenance, products and tourism diversify. Tap the phases and switch on the shocks.

12 So what? · limits · credits

What changes in practice and what the study does not say

Governments and town halls

Use the findings to plan tourism in regions with potential: routes and events that bring farmers together, access roads and support for diversification. · according to the author

Inspection and credit

The farmers ask for rules and credit lines designed for small producers: today a family agro-processing unit is assessed by the same standards as a large factory. · according to the farmers interviewed

Farmers thinking of welcoming visitors

In the accounts, tourism was a complement (“10, 15, 20% at most”, in one case) and a showcase: many sales come from people who have already visited. · our reading of the statements

Sebrae, Senac, Senai and associations

In the model, these bodies support management once the farm is set up; the state encourages relationships and collective learning. · according to the author

Limits

  • Only the farmers spoke. Public bodies and organizations were not interviewed; the author suggests hearing them to test the findings. · according to the author
  • One region, five stories. Farmers on other routes and in other states may follow another path. · according to the author
  • These are accounts, not measurements. The thesis has no figures on visitors or on the farms’ income. · our note
  • Memories told afterwards. The interviews took place in 2023, by video, about earlier years, including the pandemic ones. · our note
