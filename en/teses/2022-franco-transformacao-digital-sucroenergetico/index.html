In a sugarcane mill, digital transformation depends more on people than machines

Sensors on harvesters, big data, private networks in the cane fields: technology has reached the sugar, ethanol and bioenergy mills. The thesis interviewed 7 professionals running digital transformation programmes in the sector and found 8 critical elements for success. In 11 screens.

02 The starting point · bibliometric study

03 The method · in-depth interviews

The study interviewed 7 professionals who run digital transformation in mills

The sugar and energy sector makes sugar, ethanol and electricity from sugarcane. According to the thesis, Brazil is the world's largest sugar producer and second-largest ethanol producer. The study interviewed executives and managers from São Paulo companies in the sector with formal digital transformation programmes and projects, from January 2021 to February 2022. 09 The unexpected · cultural issues (CT06)

The harvester already sends data; why doesn't it take commands yet?

On the harvesters, sensors send data to the farm operations centre, which stores it and analyses it later. The same centre could send commands back: open a valve, start an engine, speed up a tractor. Why doesn't that happen yet?

[…] the data flow from the harvester sensors is still one-way, just for storing the data and analysing it later. […] from the operations centre we could open a valve, switch an engine on or off, and so on. […] it's possible, but it's not part of the day-to-day, it's not part of the work culture of the field staff, imagine the productivity gain we could have!?E5

10 Strategic partnerships (CT01 and PS03)

No project moved without a partner, and that comes at a price

The interviewees agreed that the in-house team lacks the expertise to change several areas at once. The answer was consultancies, specialist firms and startups. The study did not see a single project, plan or initiative without a partner relevantly involved. See the three sides:

Sections 5.7.1, 5.7.5, 5.8.3 and 5.9. The three kinds of partner come from the quotes (consultancies, specialist firms, startups bought or contracted); company names mentioned in the quotes were removed by us.

11 Takeaways

Digital transformation is a long journey, made of projects that go beyond technology

For the author, companies tend to succeed when they plan a long-term portfolio of projects that, beyond technology, takes the organization's social and cultural features into account.

For top management

  • Treat transformation as a long-term agenda tied to strategic planning, not as buying systems.
  • Drive engagement: in the sector it is rarely spontaneous and depends on each director giving time, people and focus.
  • Budget each year for the expertise the team lacks.

For those running the projects

  • Set up teams or cells dedicated to transformation within business areas.
  • Get business areas to own the solutions; without that, value is not captured.
  • Plan governance for after the projects: processes, policies and new roles.

For working with partners

  • Choose partners for their digital transformation experience and their presence with executives.
  • Protect confidentiality with contract clauses and non-disclosure agreements.
  • Use the projects to train the in-house team and reduce dependence. (our reading)

What this research does not say

  • Rules for the whole sector. The results are typical of the companies studied and cannot be generalized; they serve as real examples for managers. · according to the author
  • A picture outside the pandemic. The most intense part of the work took place during covid-19, in the companies and for the researchers. · according to the author
  • A tested model. The model and propositions are left for quantitative studies, focus groups or Delphi panels to confirm, with more people and other regions. · according to the author
  • Other voices. There were 7 people, all from São Paulo companies with formal programmes, in a single round of interviews; field workers and the partners were not interviewed. · our observation
