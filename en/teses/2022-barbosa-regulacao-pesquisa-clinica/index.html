Who makes the rules for clinical trials?

Before a new medicine reaches the pharmacy, it is tested on people, and who approves those tests in Brazil is a matter of dispute. The thesis followed, from 2015 to 2021, a bill that sought to rewrite those rules. It found a rule that does not stand still: while the law was being debated, the ethics review system changed its own norms, and those changes made their way back into the bill. Explained in 12 screens.

02 The problem · Brazil in clinical research

The same country, two portraits

Clinical research is testing a new medicine on people to prove it is safe and works. Worldwide it is a market of 112 billion dollars a year. Those who wanted to change the rules said Brazil lagged behind because of them. Those who defended the system answered with other figures. Switch the lens.

Figures cited in the thesis from the research-based pharmaceutical industry association (Interfarma, 2019 and 2020) and from the CONEP coordinator’s statement at a hearing in the Chamber of Deputies (2018). The thesis does not check them: it shows how each side picks the comparison that favors it.

03 The system · ethics and health surveillance

A test on people goes through two doors

One door is ethical: it protects the rights and safety of participants. It is run by more than 800 local committees (CEPs) and a national commission (CONEP), both tied to the National Health Council. The other is sanitary: ANVISA, the health surveillance agency, oversees the study so the product can reach the market. Choose a type of research and see the path.

Path described at the Senate hearings (2015) and in chapter 6 of the thesis. Topics that go to CONEP include human genetics, human reproduction, Indigenous peoples and research with foreign cooperation.

05 The lens · actor-network theory

In the fight, things act too

The thesis uses actor-network theory, by Bruno Latour, Michel Callon and John Law. It does not start from “who is in charge”: it follows everything that changes the course of events, human or not. A legal opinion, a disease, a set of house rules. These participants are called actants. Switch the lens and tap the points.

07 Controversy CT01.01 · the delay

Both sides counted days, but not the same days

“A person with cancer cannot wait,” senators in favor of the bill kept saying. CONEP replied that its own review time had dropped sharply. Critics countered that the first opinion had become fast but came back with dozens of pending issues, and that the whole path took more than a year. Compare on the same scale.

08 Controversies CT01.03 and CT01.07 · consent and ethical conflict

The freedom of those who take the risk, or protection for those in despair?

If research can save and can also kill, the tipping point becomes the consent of those who take part. For one side, the last word belongs to those who want to try. For the other, despair takes away the freedom to choose, and that is why the rule exists. Put arguments made at the hearings on the scale.

09 Narrative 3 · the bill

The national ethics commission moved house at every stage

10 Controversy CT02.04 · membership

Who sits at the table: from 30 seats to 15, then 17

The fight over who decides became a fight over seats. Defenders of the system spoke of social control, with users and members chosen by consensus. Supporters of the bill wanted a more technical body. Compare the three versions.

11 The finding · regulation is performative

The law never came out, yet the rule had already changed (and changed the bill back)

After four years without a new norm, the National Health Council issued six resolutions between 2016 and 2021, the first one the year after the bill. They answered the criticism. And in 2021 the Chamber brought the logic of one of them into the bill. The thesis calls this performativity: the rule acts on what it regulates and is acted on in return. Tap the points and arrows.

12 So what? · limits · credits

What changes in practice and what the study does not say

Regulators

Do not expect a law to settle the matter for good. The rule controls, but it is also controlled by those it regulates, and the “black boxes” leak. · according to the author

Lawmakers

The debate changes reality before the vote: while the bill was moving, the Council changed the very norms it sought to replace. · according to the author

Industry and research centers

Compare review times measured the same way before arguing over numbers: one body’s step and the whole path are not the same thing. · our reading

Management researchers

Describe before explaining. Mapping controversies is a way to study processes that are still in motion. · according to the author

Limits

  • Documents only. The thesis relies mainly on shorthand records and process documents; there was no observation in Congress and no ethnography. · according to the author
  • Few actants reached. Access to those who took part in the dispute was limited. · according to the author
  • The story is not over. When the thesis ended, the bill was still headed to the Chamber floor and would go back to the Senate. · according to the author
  • One slice. The focus was the ethics review system. Placebo use and post-trial treatment, also in the bill, were left out. · according to the author
  • Numbers from the debaters. Review times and rankings come from statements and cited reports; the thesis does not check them. · our note
